# English Lounge · 英语角

[English](#english) | [中文](#中文)

A DeepSeek Harness (DSH) client plugin that turns idle moments into an English micro-environment: rotating word & phrase flashcards under the composer while a response streams in, plus a right-sidebar page with topic vocabulary, a four-state word book, a quiz (spelling / multiple choice / sentence cloze / dictation), spaced-review scheduling and learning stats (words today / day streak / state distribution / heatmap) — with optional pronunciation on every card. The topic brief is temporarily hidden as of v1.7.0.

一款 DeepSeek Harness（DSH）客户端插件：在模型回复生成的等待时间里，于输入框下方轮播单词与短语闪卡；右侧栏提供主题词汇、四态单词本、小测试（拼写/四选一/句式挖空/听写）、间隔复习与学习统计（今日刷词/连续天数/状态分布/热力图），各处词卡都可选发音朗读。主题简报自 v1.7.0 起暂时隐藏。

## What's new in 1.7.0 · 本版新增

- **Spaced review · 间隔复习**: every state change now records a timestamp. Words come due on a schedule — fuzzy/new after 12 hours, known after 3 days, mastered after 21 — and due words jump the queue: the composer strip plays them first (most overdue first), and the quiz weights due words ×3 when sampling its pool. Seeing a word often is not the same as seeing it at the right time. 每次标记状态都会记下时间：模糊/生词 12 小时、认识 3 天、熟悉 21 天后到期；到期的词插队——闪卡条先播（拖得越久越靠前），小测试抽词时到期词权重 ×3。不再只是「见得多」，而是「该见的时候见」。
- **Learning stats · 学习统计 v0**: a new sidebar Stats view: words reviewed today, day streak, how many words are due now, the four-state distribution of everything you have marked (marked words only — untouched words are never counted), and a 16-week activity heatmap. 右栏新增「统计」页：今日刷词、连续天数、当前待复习数、已标记词的四态分布（只统计标记过的词）和 16 周学习热力图。看得见的进度本身就是燃料。
- **Quiz due note · 测验到期提示**: when words are due today, the quiz intro tells you how many before you start. 今天有词到期时，测验开局页会先告诉你有几个。
- **Patterns that rotate · 句式库扩容**: each topic's sentence-pattern pool grew from 3 to 8 — 120 bilingual patterns in total, 75 of them new — and the sidebar patterns block now rotates through every detected topic, showing three at a time (up to four lines), instead of cycling the same two sentences of the lead topic. The quiz's sentence-cloze questions share the same pool and get the variety for free. 每个主题的句式池从 3 条扩到 8 条（全库 120 条，新增 75 条双语句式），右栏句式块轮换遍历所有检出主题——一次 3 条、最多 4 行，不再是主话题那两句来回换；测验的句式挖空题共用同一个池，自动跟着变多样。
- **Keyboard play · 快捷键**: the quiz is fully keyboard-driven — choice questions answer with **1–4** (options carry number badges), hints via **Alt+1** IPA / **Alt+2** letter mask / **Alt+3** example / **Alt+4** meaning (dictation), **Alt+R** replays dictation, Enter submits then advances; and **/** jumps to the lookup or book search box from anywhere in the sidebar. 测验全面支持键盘：选择题按 1–4 直接作答（选项带序号）；Alt+1/2/3/4 开音标/字母骨架/例句/释义提示，Alt+R 重听听写；Enter 提交/下一题；「/」一键聚焦查词或单词本搜索框。
- **Stats visuals fixed · 统计可视化修复**: the distribution bars and heatmap now carry a brand-green fallback (the theme token they relied on doesn't resolve inside the sidebar panel — that is why the bars looked all gray and the heatmap invisible), empty heatmap days render as light-gray cells, and the count column got a fixed width so all four bars share the same track length. 分布条与热力图补了兜底配色（原主题变量在右栏不生效，之前整条隐形）：热力图换成 GitHub 贡献图双主题色板（浅色 #ebedf0→#216e39 五档，深色 #161b22→#39d353 五档，悬停描边），分布条按四态着色、计数列定宽，四条槽长度一致。热力图按参考图对齐 GitHub 贡献图：铺满面板宽度、列按周一衬砌、顶部月份锚点、左侧星期刻度、少→多图例、五档双主题色板、悬停描边 + 图下方「日期 · 当日词数」读数（自渲染信息行，不依赖系统气泡）。
- **Saved words & sentences · 收藏功能**: every word card, tail chip and sentence row now carries a ☆ — tap it and the word or sentence lands in a new sidebar 「收藏」 view (words render as full state cards, sentences as speakable rows with their source word). Saved items persist in localStorage; only the double-confirmed 「清空学习记录」 clears them. 所有词卡、尾条词条和句子后面都有 ☆：一键收进右栏新增的「收藏」视图——单词以完整词卡呈现（带四态按钮），句子以可朗读的行呈现（标注来源词）。收藏持久保存在 localStorage，只有二次确认的「清空学习记录」会清掉。
- **Patterns draw from the bank · 句式池接入词库例句**: beyond the 120 hand-written patterns, the patterns block now also draws real example sentences from the 7,999-entry bank — on-topic, 6–22 words, always with a Chinese translation, seeded per session so one chat keeps one stable pool (up to 60) that ↻ walks. Example rows carry a small chip of their source word. 句式块加了第二来源：从词库挑贴主题、有中文翻译、长度适中的真实例句，按会话稳定抽样 60 条，与手作句式合并去重后滑窗轮换；例句行带来源词小标签。
- **Color-coded states · 四态分色**: 熟悉 green / 认识 blue / 模糊 amber / 生词 red — on state buttons, on a colored left edge of each card, in the word-book filters and in the stats distribution bars (light and dark palettes). The old dim-by-opacity treatment is gone. Words explicitly marked as 生词 get the red edge too, and hovering a card names its current state. 四态按钮、卡片左缘色条、单词本筛选、统计分布条、尾条状态文字全部按状态着色（浅/深双套色值），替代原来一刀切的变暗处理；悬停词卡会显示当前状态名，色条含义一目了然。
- **Complete words, everywhere · 单词在任何控件下都完整**: some bank entries carry a very long IPA string (3–4 pronunciation variants joined by semicolons), which could squeeze the headword into `div…` in the turn-tail mini-cards — and the word itself had a hidden 26-character cap. Words now never ellipsize in any widget: cards wrap onto extra lines, long IPA continues beneath the word, and the 例 toggle wraps instead of getting crushed. 有些词条的音标特别长（分号并列 3–4 个读音变体），曾把尾条小卡里的词头挤成 `div…`；词头本身还带着一个隐藏的 26 字符宽度上限。现在任何控件下单词都不再被省略号截断：侧栏词卡与尾条小卡放不下就自动换行，音标在词后接续显示，「例」按钮也不再被挤碎；底部闪卡条保持 v1.6 起的单行约定——词条永不收缩、永不截断，过长的音标以省略号显示、悬停可看全文。
- **Topic brief hidden, patterns stay · 简报隐藏、句式保留**: the sidebar topic brief (paragraph + chips) is hidden for now — it crowded out the word list. Sentence patterns come back as their own compact block at the top of the sidebar (↻ swaps sets, speaker on every line). The brief's code stays; flip one BRIEF_ENABLED flag to bring it back. 右栏主题简报（段落+主题 chip）暂时整体隐藏——它把词表挤没了；句式学习改为独立小块保留在右栏顶部（↻ 换一组、每句可朗读）。简报代码保留，改一个 BRIEF_ENABLED 开关即可恢复。

## What's new in 1.6.0 · 本版新增

- **Richer topic brief · 主题简报更完整**: the sidebar brief is no longer one sentence — each of the 14 topics now opens with a two-to-three sentence English paragraph, a one-line *when it helps* note, and three idiomatic sentence patterns with Chinese translations (↻ swaps in another set). 右栏主题简报不再是单句：14 个主题都改成 2–3 句英文段落，附一句「适用场景」和三条带中文对照的地道句式，点 ↻ 可换一组。
- **Pronunciation · 朗读**: a speaker button now sits on every word card, the composer strip, the turn-tail cards, the sidebar patterns and the quiz answer reveal — using the browser's built-in Web Speech API, so there is no download, no network and no audio asset. It picks an en-US voice automatically, has its own on/off switch in Settings, and disappears entirely if the browser cannot speak. 词卡、闪卡条、尾条、侧栏句式、测验判分行都加了喇叭按钮，走浏览器内置 Web Speech API——不下载、不联网、不带音频文件；自动挑 en-US 音色，设置页有开关，浏览器不支持时按钮自动消失。
- **Drill the misses · 只练错词**: the quiz summary can rebuild a round from exactly the words you got wrong, instead of reshuffling everything. 结算页可以直接用本轮错词重建一轮，不必重新洗牌整池。
- **Review the words you know · 复习「认识」的词**: the quiz pulls about 20% of its questions from words you marked ○ known, tagged with a 「复习」 badge on the question — so words you already claimed to know get a real check instead of never coming back. 小测试约 20% 的题来自你标过「认识」的词，题面带「复习」角标——标过认识的词终于会被复验，而不是从此不再出现。
- **Sentence cloze · 句式挖空题**: beyond single words, the quiz takes a topic sentence pattern, blanks its most useful word, shows you the Chinese line and asks you to complete the English sentence; the full sentence is revealed when you answer. 除了单词，测验还会拿主题句式挖掉一个关键词，只给中文对照让你补全英文整句，判分后显示完整句子。
- **Dictation · 听写题**: some spelling questions become listening questions — the word is spoken once, you can replay it, and the meaning stays hidden until you ask for a hint. 部分拼写题变成听写：先听发音再拼，可重复播放，释义默认隐藏、点按钮才显示。
- **Four-state word book · 四态单词本**: the word book (formerly 生词本) now opens on fuzzy + new by default but lets you filter all four states, each button carrying its own count — so known and mastered words are no longer a dead end. 原「生词本」改名为「单词本」：默认仍筛「模糊 + 生词」，但四态按钮都可点选（可多选，各带数量），认识与熟悉的词不再是死路。
- **On-topic words, at last · 词终于是贴着话题的**: the turn-tail strip no longer surfaces stray system tokens — the snapshot's `historyLoadStatus: 'idle'` used to be read as conversation text, which is why *idle* kept coming back — and topic vocabulary is now tiered (topic-tagged words first, keyword matches second, generic words only as filler) with a 14× weight on tagged words. A conversation with no detected topic now falls back to the daily-life pool instead of the whole 6,614-word bank. 尾条不再抽系统字段：快照里的 `historyLoadStatus: 'idle'` 一直被当对话原文读，`idle` 才反复出现；主题词改为分三档取（带标签 > 命中关键词 > 通用兜底），标签词权重 14 倍；词库里那批纯语法词（from / at / not / for…）也一并剔出词池——它们出现在每段英文里，永远抢走命中名额。识别不出主题的会话退回日常话题池，不再从 6,614 条全库随机。同一回合的词还按 `session|turn` 缓存，切换对话或标完状态后不会再偷偷换一批——以前重挂一次就重抽一次，已掌握的词被滤掉、露脸计数继续累加，尾条看起来就“刷新”了。
- **Collapsible brief · 简报可收起**: the topic brief now has a small ▾ Hide / ▸ Show toggle in its header, and the choice is remembered per session state — collapsed keeps the topic chips but drops the paragraph and the pattern rows, which is what was squeezing the word list and the quiz on a laptop screen. 主题简报标题行加了 ▾ 收起 / ▸ 展开 小按钮，选择会记住：收起后只留主题 chip，段落与句式行都不渲染——右栏被简报和句式占满、下面词表和小测试没空间的问题就解决了。
- **Strip that stays one line · 单词条不再折行**: the bottom word strip was growing taller in narrow panes because the word / speaker / IPA / part-of-speech cluster was allowed to wrap inside a nowrap bar. That cluster is now a single non-wrapping line with a fixed minimum height, and every text segment ellipsises instead of pushing. 底部单词条在窄面板里会变高：外壳虽是 nowrap，但内部的「单词+喇叭+音标+词性」小行本身允许折行，四段一挤就折成两行。现在这一行固定不换行且有最小高度，各段改为溢出省略。
- **IPA that actually shows · 音标真的查得到**: the quiz's 音标 hint used to look dead — the reveal reused the word-card class, which ellipsises to zero width in the narrow quiz column, and 1,219 bank entries simply have no IPA. The reveal now has its own wrapping class, and a 374-entry phonetic table cleaned from the four bundled dictionaries fills many of the gaps (words with no IPA in a round: 31.8% → 17.8%), with stem fallback and per-word phrase composition behind it. 测验里的「音标」按钮以前像没反应：揭示行复用了词卡的 `.elz-ipa`（窄列里会被 ellipsis 压成 0 宽），而且词库有 1,219 条本来就没有音标。现在揭示行独立可换行，另从内置四本词书清洗出 374 条音标补表（一轮题目的无音标率 31.8% → 17.8%），再配复数/时态还原与词组逐词拼接。
- **Architecture pass · 架构与性能梳理**: the whole runtime got a review pass and the hot spots are cached. The same full-bank scan used to run three times per mount (strip, tail, quiz); pool lookups are now memoised per level preset, per topic+text hash and per pack revision, with one revision counter invalidating all of them when you mark a word. A 7,999-entry sort per strip render became a single three-way bucketing pass, a duplicate fallback path collapsed into one helper, the injected `<style>` node is built once, and localStorage writes are debounced to 120ms with a flush on page hide instead of one full `JSON.stringify` per click. Measured: topic pool 2.55ms → 0.004ms, strip pool 1.75ms → 0.001ms, quiz build 3.57ms → 1.3ms. 整份运行时过了一遍架构审查：同一次全库扫描以前在闪卡条/尾条/小测试里各跑一遍，现在按档位、按「主题+文本哈希」、按词包版本分别缓存，统一由标记版本号失效；闪卡条对 7999 条做全排序改成一次三路分桶；重复的兜底路径收敛成一个 helper；`<style>` 节点只构造一次；写盘从每次点击一次 `JSON.stringify` 改成 120ms 去抖 + 页面隐藏时强制落盘。实测：主题池 2.55ms → 0.004ms、闪卡池 1.75ms → 0.001ms、出题 3.57ms → 1.3ms。
- **One brief, one pronunciation slot · 简报单块化 + 朗读就位**: the topic brief is a single block led by the strongest topic (a second topic only becomes a chip), the bank total and the four-state progress strip are gone, and every card shows word → 🔊 in the same order with the redundant state badge removed. 主题简报合并为一块（以最强主题为主，第二主题只留 chip）；词库总数与四态进度条移除；所有卡片的朗读按钮统一紧贴单词右侧，卡内状态角标去掉（下方按钮已高亮）。

## Install · 安装

Requires DSH web/desktop with the shipped right sidebar (standard desktop profiles). 需要带右侧栏的标准 desktop profile。

### Option A — via the plugin market (Discover) · 通过插件市场

In DSH: **Plugins → Discover → 索引来源（Index sources）→ Add index source**, then add:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wmingzhou7-cell/DSH-English-learning/main/index.json
```

Then search “English Lounge / 英语角” and click **Install**. 在 Discover 里搜索安装即可。

### Option B — direct Git spec · 直接按 Git spec 安装

`plugin_manager` 的 `install_bundle`（或 Plugins 页安装入口）使用如下 spec（commit 以 `main` 最新为准）：

```
git+https://github.com/wmingzhou7-cell/DSH-English-learning.git#<commit-sha>
```

### Option C — local checkout · 本地目录安装

Clone this repo, then point `install_bundle` at the checkout directory. 克隆仓库后将目录交给 `install_bundle`。

## Compatibility · 兼容性

Developed on DSH `0.2.2-rc.1`, currently verified on `0.2.2-rc.3` (desktop profile, web client). The index entry declares no `engines.dsh` range; it relies on the right-sidebar tab registry (`ctx.sidebarRightTabs`) shipped in the 0.2.x line — older hosts will show the dock strip but no sidebar page. 基于 DSH 0.2.2-rc.1 开发、当前在 0.2.2-rc.3 上验证；索引未声明 engines.dsh 范围，过旧宿主仅有闪卡条而无右侧栏页。

## Privacy · 隐私

Everything runs in the browser. Topic detection uses only the session title and a bounded slice of recent session text (kept in memory, never persisted or uploaded); saved topic packs stay in browser `localStorage`. Nothing leaves the machine beyond what DSH itself already exchanges. 全部逻辑在浏览器本地运行；主题识别只用会话标题与最近会话文本的有限片段（仅在内存中，不落盘、不上传），保存的主题词包只存浏览器 localStorage；除 DSH 本身交换的内容外无任何数据离开本机。

## Implementation notes · 实现说明

- Pure client plugin: the Host half is an empty `apply()`; all rendering lives in the lazy browser module (`client.js`) with React from the host module table. 纯 Client 插件，宿主半为空。
- Mounts: `conversation.composer.dock` (session-scoped slot, per-session `inject(sessionId)`), and a right-sidebar page type via `ctx.sidebarRightTabs.register` + `sidebar.right.pane.tab` / `sidebar.right.pane.tab.title` (key = type id), opened with `ctx.sidebarRight.openTab(kind)`; a guide entry card registers the way in.
- Running state: `sessions.binding(id).session.getSnapshot().running`; title: `sessions.list` catalog snapshot.
- Styling uses only host theme tokens (`--dsw-alias-*`); literal colours appear solely in the brand glyph. 样式仅用宿主主题 token。
- No harness client package is `require`d; no DOM writes outside the component tree. 不 require 任何宿主客户端包。

Roadmap ideas: SM-2 graded intervals (v1.7.0 shipped the lightweight due-first scheduler: fixed per-state intervals + due-first ordering), a saved-pattern deck (mark sentence patterns the way you mark words), CSV import. 路线图：SM-2 间隔复习调度（v1.7.0 已落地轻量版：按状态定间隔 + 到期置顶）、可标记的句式本（把句式也纳入复习循环）、CSV 导入。（历史：主题识别与主题词包 v1.3.0 落地，v1.4.0 停用后由 v1.5.0 的 AI 选词重新驱动；四态词卡/生词本检索分页/全库查词/词库扩充 v1.4.0 落地；回复尾词条条 v1.5.0、v1.5.1 改排版；朗读/句式挖空/听写/复习池 v1.6.0 落地；间隔复习/学习统计/简报隐藏 v1.7.0 落地）
